Good evening. We begin tonight in the Make it (dy)Nasty dynasty league, where our investigative desk has spent two weeks following the money, and folks — the money leads one place. On July 6, bryansquared was the 9-seed at 42% to make the playoffs. Tonight he is the 4-seed at 66%, he owns the best first-round-bye odds in the league at 34%, and the league office is missing three rosters' worth of talent.

Here is the part that should keep the other eleven of you up at night: he didn't get hot. He didn't hit on a waiver claim. He made three trades in fourteen days — and his three trade partners lost a combined 35 points of playoff odds while he pocketed 24 of them. ljwestwood, down 10. brochillington, down 3. And this morning at 11:53 AM, while zhyman was presumably on his third meeting of the workday, the man walked out of his roster with George Kittle, Jonathan Taylor, AND James Cook. zhyman: down 22 points before noon. That is not trade activity. That is a redistribution of wealth with a group chat attached.

But every wildfire has an ignition point, and our timeline puts patient zero on July 13 — when brochillington, two weeks before training camp, publicly challenged this league's commitment to roster management. He did it the way all great commissioners of chaos do: with a photograph of the only member of his household maintaining a professional offseason routine.

Then this Monday happened. The numbers below are sworn testimony.

And one more thing before the evidence table, because our desk found it at 2 AM and hasn't slept since: this didn't start in July. Back on May 14, bryansquared quietly acquired Brian Thomas Jr. and Oronde Gadsden II from CharmCity20. Both of those players were later shipped out as ammunition in the July trades. That's not activity, folks. That's premeditation. He wasn't building a roster in May — he was loading the weapon.
