The offseason already had a winner

Good evening. Or good whatever hour it is when you're reading this on your phone in a parking lot. Sleeper says the season starts Wednesday. The league's own numbers say the offseason already had a winner, and it isn't who the July edition would have guessed.

Forty-seven days ago this desk ran a story called "Blood on His Hands." Three of the twelve rows on that odds board have moved ten points or more since. Two of those three are the two names the July story was built around, and each went the other way.

Start at the bottom of the July board and work up. zhyman was the ten seed at 32 percent to make the playoffs. Tonight he is the eight seed at 47. That is plus fifteen, the largest move on the board in either direction, and seven and a half times the page's stated error margin. His transaction log for the window contains one entry. On July 21 he released a kicker. That is the whole log. No trades, no claims, no bids. The kicker was Cairo Santos. We are not saying these two facts are related. We are saying they are in the same paragraph.

The July buyer went the other way. bryansquared was the four seed at 66 percent with the best bye odds in the league at 34. He is now the six seed at 56, bye odds 25, title odds down from 12 to 8. And here is the part the desk found while pulling the ledger. The three players he pried out of zhyman on July 20, Jonathan Taylor, James Cook, and George Kittle, are all gone. Taylor went to mattkendall1 on August 20 for Breece Hall, Christian Watson, and two 2028 thirds. Cook and Kittle went to DD00GZ on August 23, with a 2028 third, for De'Von Achane, Chris Godwin, and a 2029 fifth. The Monday Morning Massacre lasted thirty-four days. FantasyCalc scores the Taylor deal as more value coming back to him (7,955 to 5,599). It scores the Achane deal the other way (7,796 received, 8,574 sent). KeepTradeCut's page feed, which carries no picks, calls the second one a coin flip: 10,026 to 9,973.

The biggest trade of the window by total value wasn't his. On August 11, CharmCity20 and chrisapplegate moved 24,997 points of FantasyCalc value between them, the most of any deal since July. CharmCity20 got Drake London, Kenneth Walker, Dallas Goedert, and chrisapplegate's 2028 first. chrisapplegate got Trey McBride, DeVonta Smith, and Aaron Rodgers. FantasyCalc gives CharmCity20 the edge, 13,333 to 11,664. KeepTradeCut has it 15,113 to 14,740. Rotowire's season projections on Sleeper lean the other way, 641 points to 630. Three sources, two winners, one conclusion: it was close, and everyone involved will tell you otherwise. Since then chrisapplegate sits at 78 percent, still the one seed, and his bye odds went from 31 to 38. That plus seven is the largest bye gain on the board.

About CharmCity20. Thirteen trades cleared in the window. He was in six of them. On August 8 the league completed four trades in seven hours; he was in one. On August 13 it completed three trades in eighty-three minutes; he was in two of those, one minute apart at 10:06 and 10:07 PM.

The waiver wire, where dynasty leagues go to feel productive. Fifty-four pickups since July 20, every one of them a waiver claim, and the most valuable of them by FantasyCalc was free. Keenan Allen, value 1,031, was dropped by brochillington on July 24 and claimed by bryansquared on July 31 for zero dollars. Third on the list, Najee Harris, was dropped by ljwestwood on July 30 and claimed by DD00GZ on August 20, also for zero. Fourth, Pat Freiermuth, dropped by ljwestwood August 5, claimed by itsders August 7 at zero; ChessNerd420's claim on the same player failed the same morning. The pattern here isn't complicated. The best players on the wire were on rosters in this league two weeks earlier.

The actual money went elsewhere. The two largest winning bids of the window were both 101 dollars: DD00GZ on Darren Waller, and itsders on Xavier Hutchinson, whom FantasyCalc ranks 321st. Same price, 84 ranking spots apart. brochillington spent the most in the window, 149 dollars across nine claims, including 69 each on Haynes King and Charlie Kolar. bryansquared won eleven claims, the most in the league, and had five claims fail, also the most in the league.

The rest of the board, briefly. DD00GZ, four trades and six claims deep, is at 77 percent, up four, and his semifinal odds went from 45 to 49. Lupeeeeeeee's bye odds went from 23 to 28 on two trades and zero claims. mattkendall1's playoff odds didn't move at all, 66 and 66, while his bye odds went from 25 to 18 and his semifinal odds from 56 to 45, tied with bryansquared for the biggest semifinal drop on the page. ljwestwood went from 44 to 36 and from the nine seed to the ten. And brochillington went from 15 percent to 5 and from eleventh to twelfth, on three trades and nine claims. The desk was told to report the numbers. These are the numbers.

And one more thing. This league's trade deadline, per the settings page on Sleeper, is week 99. The playoffs start in week 15. There is no deadline. Thirteen trades in forty-seven days, and it's the part of the calendar where nothing happens. Sleep well.
